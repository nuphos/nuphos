Chats come to iPhone: a Chats tab beside Agents, with unread dots, Messages-style motion, and members' avatars as a chat's icon. Post in a chat, and agents' files show as download cards.

The chat's dock is on your iPhone, mirrored from the Mac, and a Mac's localhost and LAN pages open natively.

Invite someone to a conversation only if they can use its computer; people who can use the computer can reply.

Sign-in is simpler: enter your email, then a six-digit code, or continue with Google or Apple directly. Switch workspace from the Account sheet.

Fixes: every chat opens, not only the first one after launch. A chat or conversation you have open is marked read, a chat post keeps the line breaks you typed, and the keyboard's return key adds a new line again.
