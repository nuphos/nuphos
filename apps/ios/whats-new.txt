- Switch agents inside a session from the chat menu, and see the pull requests and issues linked to it.
- Threads an agent opens are nested under the conversation that opened them. Fold them, or unlink one.
- Image preview: swipe past the last image to reach the first, pinch to zoom, and pull down to close.
- The keyboard follows your finger when you pull down on the composer or the conversation, and stays with the back swipe.
- In a shared session, anyone who can reply can steer or stop the running turn.
